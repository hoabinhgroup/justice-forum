Saturday, 10 October 2026

Hosted by Viet Nam

Held alongside the official Forum programme and after the Forum

Venues: Da Nang Ball Room 1, Han River 1, Han River 2
Hosts: H.E. Nguyen Van Quang, Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court of Viet Nam; H.E. Nguyen Van Tien, Deputy Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court of Viet Nam; H.E. Pham Quoc Hung, Deputy Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court of Viet Nam

Time Activity Meeting room
Morning: Bilateral meetings hosted by Chief Justice Nguyen Van Quang with the delegations of China and Brunei Darussalam
10:00 – 10:30 Bilateral meeting between Chief Justice Nguyen Van Quang and the Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court of China Da Nang Ball Room 1
11:15 – 11:45 Bilateral meeting between Chief Justice Nguyen Van Quang and the Chief Justice of the Supreme Court of Brunei Darussalam
(Brunei Darussalam’s presentation concludes at 11:00)		 Da Nang Ball Room 1
Afternoon: Bilateral meetings hosted by Chief Justice Nguyen Van Quang with the delegations of Timor-Leste and Myanmar
14:00 – 14:30 Bilateral meeting between Chief Justice Nguyen Van Quang and the Chief Justice of the Supreme Court of Myanmar
(Myanmar delivers its presentation in the morning and has no presentation scheduled in the afternoon)		 Da Nang Ball Room 1
14:45 – 15:15 Bilateral meeting between Chief Justice Nguyen Van Quang and the Chief Justice of the Court of Appeal of Timor-Leste (Timor-Leste has no presentation scheduled in the afternoon, and its bilateral meeting with China concludes at 14:30) Da Nang Ball Room 1
After the Forum: Bilateral meetings hosted by Deputy Chief Justice Nguyen Van Tien and Deputy Chief Justice Pham Quoc Hung with the delegations of Indonesia and Malaysia
17:30 – 18:00 Deputy Chief Justice Pham Quoc Hung meets with the delegation of the Supreme Court of Indonesia Han River 1
17:30 – 18:00 Deputy Chief Justice Nguyen Van Tien meets with the delegation of the Supreme Court of Malaysia Han River 2
End of the bilateral meeting programme

Saturday, 10 October 2026

Hosted by China

Held alongside the official Forum programme

Venues: Da Nang Ball Room 1, Han River 1
Host: H.E. Zhang Jun, President and Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court of China

Time Activity Meeting room
Morning: Bilateral meetings hosted by the Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court of China with the delegations of Viet Nam and Brunei Darussalam
10:00 – 10:30 Bilateral meeting between the Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court of China and the Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court of Viet Nam Da Nang Ball Room 1
12:00 – 12:30 Bilateral meeting between the Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court of China and the Chief Justice of the Supreme Court of Brunei Darussalam Han River 1
Afternoon: Bilateral meetings hosted by the Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court of China with the delegations of Timor-Leste and Indonesia
14:00 – 14:30 Bilateral meeting between the Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court of China and the Chief Justice of the Court of Appeal of Timor-Leste (Timor-Leste delivers its presentation in the morning only and has no presentation scheduled in the afternoon) Han River 1
15:20 – 15:50 Bilateral meeting between the Chief Justice of the Supreme People’s Court of China and the delegation of the Supreme Court of Indonesia (Indonesia’s afternoon presentation concludes at 15:05) Han River 1
End of the bilateral meeting programme
